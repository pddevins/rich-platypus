I'm Patrick. I have a bachelor's in philosophy, which is a strange credential to
bring to a homelab, and it's been more useful than the CV suggests.

The habit is what stuck: find the actual claim, then find what it's resting on.
That works the same on a vendor's uptime figure as it did on anything I read for
my thesis.

What I write about: Proxmox, and the music, film, and file storage I'd rather run
myself. Small electronics — Arduino, Raspberry Pi, LoRa, and enough soldering to
have opinions about flux. Privacy, which for me means threat surfaces and
security assessments as much as digital rights and anti-surveillance. Development
workflows, and how much of mine now runs on AI tooling. And consulting and
leadership: how teams make decisions, and what makes the good ones repeatable.

Long posts, short essays, build logs, and notes — all of it in the
[feed](/feed.xml).
